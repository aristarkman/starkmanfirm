Our commitment

Starkman Firm aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. That means readable contrast, text that resizes without breaking the page, keyboard access to every control, meaningful headings, labelled form fields, and descriptive alternative text for images.
