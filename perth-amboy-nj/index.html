The Perth Amboy office

Our Perth Amboy office serves Middlesex, Union, Monmouth and Ocean counties, with a heavy load of workers' compensation matters from the port, the warehouses along Route 1 and the construction sites across the waterfront. Consultations are handled in English and Spanish.

Getting here

188 Market Street is a few blocks from City Hall and within walking distance of the Perth Amboy train station on the North Jersey Coast Line. Street parking is available nearby. If you cannot travel because of an injury, we will arrange a phone meeting or come to you.

What we handle from this office

  • Workers' compensation — warehouse, port, construction, healthcare and municipal workers
  • Construction site injuries, including third-party claims against general contractors
  • Car and truck accidents on Route 35, Route 9, the Garden State Parkway and the Turnpike
  • Slip and fall claims against stores, landlords and public entities
  • Municipal court matters in Middlesex County
