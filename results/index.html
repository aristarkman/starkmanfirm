How to read case results

Settlement and verdict figures are gross recoveries before attorney's fees, costs and any medical liens. Two cases with identical injuries can resolve for very different amounts depending on available insurance, fault, and how the injury affected that particular person's work and life. Anyone who quotes you a number based on a headline figure is guessing.
